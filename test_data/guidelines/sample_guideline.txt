BRAND GUIDELINE - GreenGlow Skincare

PROHIBITED WORDS:
- "miracle" (use: "effective" or "clinically proven")
- "cure" (use: "treat" or "helps with")
- "100% effective" (use: "results may vary")
- "drug" in product context

APPROVED TERMINOLOGY:
- Products: "GreenGlow Cleansing Milk", "GreenGlow Tea Tree Face Wash", "GreenGlow Moisturizer Plus"
- Company: "GreenGlow Skincare" (NOT "GreenGlow" alone in formal context)
- Claims: Use only with evidence from approved facts

REQUIRED DISCLAIMERS:
- All skincare products: "Results may vary. Individual skin types may respond differently."
- Claims of certification: "Dermatologist-tested" only with evidence from Product Certifications table

TONE:
- Professional but approachable
- Avoid medical terminology without context
- Use inclusive language

VISUAL GUIDELINES:
- Logo: Only latest version (v3.2) approved
- Colors: Primary Green #2D5F3F, Secondary Light Green #B8E6D5
- Fonts: Headers - Inter Bold, Body - Open Sans Regular
- Images: Only product photos from approved library or natural ingredient imagery
- Contrast: Minimum WCAG AA (4.5:1 for text)

AVOID:
- Before/after photos without consent forms
- Unverified testimonials
- Expired certifications
